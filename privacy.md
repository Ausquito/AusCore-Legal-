# AusCore Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 11 November 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how AusCore processes information when it is used
in a Discord server. AusCore is an independent Discord application and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc.

## Information AusCore processes

AusCore processes only the information needed to provide the features enabled
by a server's administrators. This can include:

- Discord user, guild, role, channel, message, and interaction identifiers;
- usernames, display names, avatars, and server membership information supplied
  through the Discord API;
- feature data such as XP, message counts, voice-activity duration, achievement
  progress, streaks, counting activity, temporary-room ownership, and configured
  role rewards;
- virtual-economy balances, transactions, inventory, and game results where a
  server enables AusEconomy;
- member preferences such as timezone, reminders, AFK status, and optional
  birthday month/day and privacy choice; and
- moderation, verification, Anti-Nuke, quarantine, honeypot, and audit records
  needed to protect a server and operate its configured security features.

AusCore does not record voice audio. It does not read or log private direct
messages between Discord users. It does not collect passwords, payment-card
details, or real-world addresses.

## Server activity logs

Server administrators may configure AusCore activity logging. Depending on the
categories they enable, those logs can record Discord server events such as
member, channel, role, invite, voice-state, moderation, or AutoMod changes.
Where a server enables message-content logging, a limited staff-visible message
excerpt may be recorded for that server's moderation or audit purpose. AusCore
does not copy voice audio.

## How information is used

AusCore uses information to:

- respond to commands and interactions;
- operate the modules enabled in a server;
- prevent spam, fraud, exploits, raids, and unauthorised server changes;
- maintain configured moderation, verification, and audit workflows;
- deliver server-configured notifications, rewards, and reminders; and
- diagnose, secure, and improve the reliability of AusCore.

## Sharing and disclosure

AusCore does not sell personal information. Information is processed through
Discord's API to provide AusCore's features. It may also be processed by the
hosting or infrastructure providers used to operate AusCore. Information may be
disclosed where required by law or where necessary to investigate abuse,
security incidents, or violations of applicable rules.

## Retention and deletion

AusCore retains data only for as long as it is needed to provide the relevant
feature, meet security and moderation needs, resolve disputes, or comply with
applicable legal obligations.

Members can request deletion of their eligible personal AusCore data through
**/profile → My Settings → My Data**. Some security, moderation, verification,
and audit records may need to be retained where necessary to protect a server,
prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Public suggestions may be anonymised
or redacted through AusCore's review process rather than silently deleting
other members' discussion history.

Server owners can request removal of their server's AusCore data by using the
server data controls provided by AusCore.

## Your choices and requests

You may use the available profile controls to manage supported preferences,
including birthday privacy where enabled. For a privacy question, correction,
or request that cannot be completed in AusCore, contact
[privacy@auspanel.dev](mailto:privacy@auspanel.dev).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when AusCore changes or when required by law. The
latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
